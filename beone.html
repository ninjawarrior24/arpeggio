About the Partnership

Arpeggio Resources is proud to partner with BeOne Sports, an innovative sports technology company on a mission to make elite athletic training accessible to every athlete, at every level. Together, we are combining our expertise in immersive technology, business strategy, and digital media to help BeOne Sports grow its platform and reach new audiences.

What BeOne Sports Does

BeOne Sports uses computer vision and machine learning to analyze an athlete's biomechanics using nothing more than a standard smartphone — eliminating the need for expensive wearables or motion capture labs. Their core technology enables Comparative Training, where users record themselves and receive instant, data-driven feedback by comparing their form and technique against a proprietary catalog of movements from collegiate, professional, and Olympic athletes.

Why It Matters

The company's mission is to flatten the learning curve for athletes of all levels by providing affordable, accessible, and personalized coaching insights — maximizing potential and minimizing injury risk. Arpeggio brings immersive media, go-to-market strategy, and partnership development to accelerate their growth.
